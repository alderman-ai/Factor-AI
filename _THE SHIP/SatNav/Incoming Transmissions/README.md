# Incoming Transmissions

The intake. Everything lands here first, raw and unrefined.

Two things write into this folder:

- **`Ignition Codes/pumpjack.py`** — pulls Claude Code session transcripts out
  of its own storage, where they are outside the repo and subject to cleanup.
  Filename: `YYYY-MM-DD_HHMM_<session-short>.md`.
- **the `science` skill** — files one captured lesson, with who invoked it, why,
  and the lesson in full self-contained detail.

Nothing here has been triaged. Triage sends an entry to `Knowledge Base/`,
`Unstructured Entries/`, or `Delete/`.

Accretes. No INDEX — timestamped and homogeneous, so the filename is the
routing.
